# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in the Xeck project to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful.
- Discuss ideas and code; do not attack people.
- Accept constructive feedback and focus on what is best for the project.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. The owner will review reports and may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation. The project currently has no separate moderation team.
